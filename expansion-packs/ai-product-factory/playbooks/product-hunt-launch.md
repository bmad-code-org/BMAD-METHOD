# Product Hunt Launch Playbook

Guide for `bmad-apf-product-hunt` agent.

## Timeline

### T-7 days
- [ ] Finalize Product Hunt listing copy
- [ ] Prepare maker comment
- [ ] Create launch day social content
- [ ] Notify supporters to upvote on launch day
- [ ] Schedule email to existing users

### T-3 days
- [ ] Submit Product Hunt listing (schedule for launch day)
- [ ] Prepare GIF/video demo
- [ ] Write maker story (personal, authentic)
- [ ] Prepare FAQ responses

### T-1 day
- [ ] Final review of all assets
- [ ] Test all links
- [ ] Brief team on launch day roles
- [ ] Schedule social posts

### Launch Day
- [ ] Go live at 12:01 AM PT
- [ ] Post maker comment immediately
- [ ] Share on Twitter, LinkedIn, Reddit
- [ ] Respond to every comment within 1 hour
- [ ] Email supporters
- [ ] Monitor ranking throughout the day

### T+1 day
- [ ] Thank supporters
- [ ] Analyze traffic and signups
- [ ] Document learnings
- [ ] Follow up with new users

## Listing Copy Template

**Tagline:** (60 chars max) One sentence value prop

**Description:**
- What it does (2 sentences)
- Key features (3-5 bullets)
- Who it's for
- What makes it different

**Maker Comment:**
Personal story — why you built this, the problem you faced, invite feedback.

## Success Metrics

- Top 5 Product of the Day
- 200+ upvotes
- 50+ comments
- Measurable signup spike
