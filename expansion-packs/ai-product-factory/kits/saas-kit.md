# SaaS Product Kit

Use this kit for a SaaS experiment, MVP, or production delivery.

1. Validate the opportunity and price assumptions before committing to a build.
2. Plan product and delivery with canonical BMAD ownership.
3. Add authentication, payments, data, and observability only if the approved slice requires them.
4. Use `bmad-apf-release-product` for production readiness and rollback decisions.
