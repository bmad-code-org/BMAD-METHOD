# YC Startup Playbook — Summary for AI Product Factory

Key principles from Y Combinator applied to the founder layer agents.

## Make Something People Want

- Talk to users before writing code
- Launch fast, iterate faster
- Do things that don't scale initially

## Idea Validation Checklist

1. **Is it a real problem?** — Can you find 10 people who have this problem today?
2. **Will they pay?** — Not "would use" but "would pay"
3. **Can you reach them?** — Do you know where your users are?
4. **Why now?** — What changed in the last 2 years?
5. **Why you?** — Unfair advantage (domain expertise, network, technical edge)

## MVP Scope Rules

- Build the smallest thing that delivers core value
- Cut features until it hurts, then cut one more
- Manual processes are fine for first 100 users
- Perfect is the enemy of launched

## Metrics That Matter (Pre-PMF)

- Weekly active users (growth rate)
- Retention (D1, D7, D30)
- NPS or qualitative feedback
- Revenue (if applicable)

## Common Failure Modes

- Building in isolation without user feedback
- Premature scaling
- Solving a problem nobody has
- Feature creep before launch
- Optimizing tech stack instead of product-market fit

## Applied to APF Agents

- `bmad-apf-idea-validator` — enforces "make something people want"
- `bmad-apf-feature-prioritizer` — enforces ruthless MVP scoping
- `bmad-apf-startup-coach` — prevents premature optimization
