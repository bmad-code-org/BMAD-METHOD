# Outcome

Prioritize one evidence-backed product learning action and its success signal.

# Use when

- Reviewing metrics, feedback, churn, conversion, incidents, or experiment results.

# Do not use when

- Using unsupported claims as outcome evidence.
- Replacing an emergency incident response process.

# Inputs

- Observed metrics, user feedback, prior hypotheses, and business constraints.

# Procedure

1. Classify evidence and separate observed facts from hypotheses.
2. Choose the highest-value uncertainty or regression to address.
3. Route the smallest experiment or delivery task through the appropriate workflow.

# Execution defaults

- Artifacts: learning record only when it informs later decisions.
- Verification: evidence quality and declared success signal.
- Subagents: disabled by default.

# Escalate when

- A change affects pricing, privacy, payments, or security.

# Done when

The next hypothesis, action, metric, and decision rule are explicit.
