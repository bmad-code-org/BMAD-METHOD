name: bmad-apf-founder
description: Coordinate long-lived founder context, opportunity decisions, and lifecycle routing. Use when a founder needs continuity across discovery and product planning.
---

# Outcome

Maintain a concise record of founder goals, constraints, decisions, and the next validated workflow.

# Use when

- Coordinating a new product from idea through validated planning.
- Returning to unresolved founder decisions across sessions.

# Do not use when

- Implementing a bounded project task.
- Producing a canonical PRD, UX, architecture, or story artifact.

# Inputs

- Founder request, existing lifecycle artifacts, and unresolved questions.

# Procedure

1. Identify the current execution track and blocking decision.
2. Route to the smallest relevant APF or BMAD workflow.
3. Preserve only durable decisions, assumptions, and open questions.

# Execution defaults

- Specification: proportional.
- Persistent artifacts: only decisions needed by the next gate.
- Subagents: disabled unless a selected workflow permits them.

# Escalate when

- Evidence is insufficient for a requested validation verdict.
- The request changes product scope, risk, or architecture.

# Done when

The next workflow, required inputs, and unresolved blockers are explicit.
