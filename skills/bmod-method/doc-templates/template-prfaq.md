# {title}

## Press release
<!-- required. Written as if the product has shipped: a headline a customer would understand; a subheadline naming who benefits and what changes for them; an opening paragraph announcing it, for whom and why they should care; the problem as the customer feels it today, with no mention of the solution; the solution as what changes for the customer, outcomes not features; a leader's quote with the vision beyond the feature list; how it works from the customer's side; a customer quote a real person would say; and how to get started, concrete enough to act on. -->

## Customer FAQ
<!-- required. Six to ten questions a busy, skeptical customer would ask, hardest first: skepticism (why this over what I use today), trust (my data, who is behind this, what if it shuts down), practical concerns (cost, time to start, what it works with), edge cases, and the one question the team hopes nobody asks. "How do I sign up?" is not one. Each answer honest and specific; a gap an answer reveals is named as a launch blocker, a fast follow or an accepted trade-off. -->

## Internal FAQ
<!-- required. Six to ten questions the builder's side would ask, hardest first: feasibility (the hardest technical problem, what nobody knows how to build yet, dependencies), viability (unit economics, the first hundred customers, how durable the moat is), resources (team, realistic timeline, what gets said no to), risk (what kills this, the worst case after shipping, legal or regulatory exposure), strategic fit (why us, why now, what it cannibalizes), and the question the founder avoids. Calibrated to the user: a solo founder is not asked about board alignment, an enterprise team is not asked about weekend viability. "We'll figure it out" is not an answer; an honest unknown names what it would take to find out and by when. -->

## Verdict
<!-- required, written last. In narrative, not a score: what is solid, what needs more work before a PRD, and what could sink the concept, each weakness with what it would take to fix. Direct; the point is to surface the truth before resources are committed. -->
