# Craft Checklist

Run this pass on woven drafts, talk scripts, essays, and finished sections after `scripts/creative_lint.py`. Fix failures directly in the text; do not explain around them.

## Attention and structure

1. Open on concrete stakes or a vivid scene rather than greetings, credentials, or throat-clearing (unless an educational/reference format deliberately opens with orientation).
2. Cash every abstraction within one sentence using a real number, named case, or observable scene — never stack vague claims without concrete grounding.
3. Open a real question early and close that loop explicitly before the finish so the piece carries forward pull.
4. Announce list counts up front when enumerating ("three constraints", "two shifts") rather than springing unlabeled lists.
5. Plant callbacks so closing turns echo concrete details established earlier in the piece.

## Voice and rhythm

6. Unpack insider jargon inline in one plain phrase ("X just means Y") whenever the target audience in `{record_folder}/audience.md` does not live inside that term.
7. Prefer natural connective glue (`So`, `And`, `But`, `Now`) over formal transitions; watch out for `However,` and `Therefore,` in spoken drafts.
8. Repeat parallel sentence stems deliberately for rhetorical rhythm rather than swapping in stiff synonyms for "elegant variation".
9. Keep comparisons and analogies grounded in lived reality the audience touches every week, never generic metaphors (`journey`, `landscape`, `symphony`).
10. Alternate sentence lengths with 3-to-6-word punch lines after dense stretches; never leave 5 or more 20+ word sentences back to back.
11. Pair one memorable contrast per major turn ("minutes, not quarters") and escalate specificity toward the end of a story instead of peaking early.
12. If the piece ends with a CTA or ask, remove friction right before it: end on clarity and ease, not hectoring pressure.
