# Writing, voice, and stories

All guided through Clio (`bmad-creative-agent-director`).

- Capture unpolished thinking first -> `BRAINDUMP` (one question at a time, fragments and slang preserved in `braindumps/`).
- Turn a braindump into a finished talk script, essay, or draft -> `WEAVE`: first an approved outline gate (3 Target-Transformation-Stakes hooks + cited beats + `GAP` flags), then Quote-or-Cut drafting (`[INVENTED]` tags capped at 25%, hook written last, checked by linter and craft checklist).
- Lock down the user's real voice -> `VOICE` builds `voice-bible.md` (project-local or `~/.bmad/voice-bible.md` for cross-project personal use) from 3 to 5 real pieces in `exemplars/`, and can wire it into `bmad-facilitate` (`workflow.voice_guide` via `bmad-customize`) when other first-person docs should use it.
- Strip AI tells and filler from any existing draft -> `DESLOP`.
- Shape titles, hooks, blurbs, abstracts, and CTAs that the draft actually pays off -> `PACKAGE`.
- Capture taste feedback so misses never recur -> `RETRO` writes surgical, ISO-dated rules into the voice bible (`voice-bible.md` or the configured `voice_guide`), `blacklist.md`, or `canon.md`.
- Coached document templates in `bmad-facilitate` -> `story` (Beat Board HTML), `character` (printable 1-page Character Sheet HTML), `world` (World Almanac HTML), and `talk-script` (Teleprompter & Beat Timeline HTML).
- Loose sparks, raw transcripts, or finished pieces -> `INTAKE`.
