# Data room check

The outcome is `{record_folder}/data-room.md`: what an investor at this stage will ask to see, what exists and where, what is missing, what is out of date, and where two documents disagree. An investor in diligence who finds a gap or two different revenue figures slows down or walks, so the gaps are found before they are.

## What you cannot infer

- The list scales with the stage, from the deck, cap table and founder IP assignment at pre-seed to monthly financials and cohorts at Series A.
- Look in the project for what exists before asking. A document's metrics are stale when they are older than the latest Metrics date in `company.md`; any figure that differs from `company.md` is a disagreement to resolve with the founder.
- Legal documents you cannot judge are listed for their lawyer, not assessed.

## Hand back

The three gaps to close first, and how long the list is in total.
