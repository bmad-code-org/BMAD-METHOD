# Meeting prep and debrief

The outcome, before a call with an investor, funder or customer, is a one-page brief the founder reads in five minutes: who they are and what they care about, the history so far, the one outcome the founder wants from this call, the questions they are likely to ask with the founder's best answers, and anything not to say. After the call, the outcome is the record updated so the next step is not lost.

## What you cannot infer

- Prep reads the person's file in `{record_folder}/people/`, `company.md` and `{record_folder}/objections.md`. Public facts about the person or firm come from a quick web search, marked as such; never invent a portfolio company or a quote.
- Debrief: the notes go through `references/intake.md`. Then update the person's `Where it stands:` line with a dated next step, and add each hard question they asked to `objections.md` with the answer the founder gave and a better one if there is one.
- A question asked three times in `objections.md` belongs in the pitch itself.
- A call that ended with no next step is said plainly: it is a stall, not a maybe.

## Hand back

Prep: the brief and the one outcome to get. Debrief: where it stands now, the next step and its date.
