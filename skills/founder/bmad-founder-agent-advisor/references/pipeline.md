# Pipeline

The outcome is a ranked target list the founder works this week: investors for a raise, or the first customers for a launch, each with why they fit, the warm path to them if there is one, and the next step. Each target is a `{record_folder}/people/` file, so meetings, check-ins and outreach read the same list.

## What you cannot infer

- Investor fit is stage, check size, thesis, recent deals and portfolio conflicts; a fund that has never led at this stage is not a lead. Customer fit is the ideal customer in `insights.md` or the `ideal-customer` document when there is one.
- With no list, research one through `bmad-deep-recon`; say so before it starts, since it takes a while. Never invent a firm, a partner or a deal.
- Rank by fit and warmth together; a warm intro to a good fit beats a cold email to a perfect one.

## Hand back

The top five with the next step for each, and how many of the rest are worth contacting at all.
