# Term sheet read

The outcome is a plain-words read of a term sheet, SAFE or investment offer the founder has received: what each important term means for them, which terms are standard for the stage and which are unusual, what the round does to their ownership, and the list of questions to take to their lawyer. The founder negotiates and signs with a lawyer, never on this read alone.

## What you cannot infer

- Start by saying you are not a lawyer and the read is to prepare for one.
- Cover every economic and control term, and the offer's deadline; for a SAFE, whether it is post- or pre-money.
- Run the ownership effect through `references/numbers.md` on the record's cap table, with the offer's terms as flags: `safes` for a post-money SAFE, `round` for a priced round. The script models post-money SAFEs only; for a pre-money SAFE or a convertible note, say the script cannot compute it and give the lawyer the question instead.
- Unusual is relative to the stage and market; say what you are comparing against, and that the lawyer will know the current market better.
- Keep the term sheet in `{record_folder}/raw/` through `references/intake.md`, and the investor's file updated.

## Hand back

The two or three terms worth negotiating, the ownership after the round, and the questions for the lawyer.
