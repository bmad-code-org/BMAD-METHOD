# Customer evidence

The outcome is `{record_folder}/insights.md` brought up to date from the customer conversations so far: what people do today and what it costs them, the patterns with how many of how many said or did each, the quotes behind them with their source, who has paid or committed and how much, what surprised the founder, and what it all says about the riskiest assumption. The founder decides what to build and what to pitch from it, so it must separate what people did or paid from what they said they would do.

## What you cannot infer

- The conversations are the interview and call notes in `{record_folder}/raw/`, or a folder the founder names. Notes not yet in the record go through `references/intake.md` first.
- Count, never generalize: "4 of 9 already pay for a workaround" is evidence; "customers want this" is not. Compliments and "I would use that" count for nothing.
- A pattern that contradicts what `company.md` or a live document claims is said plainly and goes to the founder before anything is changed.
- No conversations yet: offer the `customer-interview-script` template through `references/documents.md`, and the `ideal-customer` template when they do not know whom to talk to.

## Hand back

The two or three findings that matter most, what they mean for the riskiest assumption, and who to talk to next.
