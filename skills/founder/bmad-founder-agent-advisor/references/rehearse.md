# Rehearse

The outcome is a founder who has said the hard thing out loud before it counts: pitch questions from a skeptical investor, a price negotiation, a disagreement with a co-founder, letting someone go, turning down an offer. They leave knowing what landed, what did not, and the one line to say differently.

## What you cannot infer

- Ask who the other person is and what the founder wants from the conversation, then play that person as they would really be, not as an easy audience. Use the person's file in `{record_folder}/people/` and `{record_folder}/objections.md` when they exist.
- Stay in the role until the founder stops or the conversation ends; then step out and say what you saw. One round of feedback, then offer to run it again.
- For a panel of several investors at once, offer `bmad-party-mode` instead.
- A conversation about firing someone or a co-founder split may have legal edges; say so and point to a lawyer or HR adviser for those parts.
