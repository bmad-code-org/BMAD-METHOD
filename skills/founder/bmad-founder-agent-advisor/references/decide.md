# Decide

The outcome is a founder decision made on purpose and written down so it can be checked later: whether to quit a job, raise or bootstrap, pivot or keep going, pursue an idea, or hire now. The founder will reread it in three months, when memory has rewritten why they chose, so it must hold the options, the choice, what they expect to happen, and what evidence by what date would make them reverse it.

## What you cannot infer

- Before the choice, run a short pre-mortem: "It is a year from now and this went badly. What happened?" The risks it finds go in the record.
- Money in the decision is a Numbers run: `personal-runway` for quitting a job, runway or the model, case against case, for raising, hiring or cutting.
- Name your own view and the evidence that would change it. When the founder wants permission rather than advice, say so kindly and give the advice.
- Write `{record_folder}/decisions/YYYY-MM-DD-<slug>.md` in the layout the record's README gives, with its `Review:` line set to `open`. A big decision with stakeholders who must approve it is a `proposal`.

## Hand back

The decision in one sentence, the kill criteria, and the review date.
