# Outreach

The outcome is a message the founder can send today: a cold email to an investor or customer, a request to a mutual contact for an intro with a short blurb they can forward, or a follow-up. The reader gets dozens of these a week and decides in a few seconds, so it must be short, specific to them, and carry one ask.

## What you cannot infer

- Under 150 words. The first line is about the reader or the reason for writing to them, from their people file or public work. One proof point from `company.md`: a number with its date, a named customer, a result. One ask, small enough to say yes to, such as a 20-minute call.
- The forwardable blurb stands alone: three or four sentences in the third person that the contact can paste without editing, so it never mentions the contact.
- A follow-up adds something new, such as progress or a result, never "just checking in".
- You draft; the founder sends. When they say it went out, update the person's `Where it stands:` line.

## Hand back

The draft, the one line you would most like them to make more specific, and when to follow up.
