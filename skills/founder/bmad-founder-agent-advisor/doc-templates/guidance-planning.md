# Business planning guidance

For founder plans, diagnostics, and proposals. These rules add to the engine's; the template's own comments win over them.

## Stakes

Read the stakes as one of: hobby or personal use, internal, pitch or investor, publication, public launch, regulated or contracted.

- At hobby stakes, one line fills a section. Skip reconciliation unless the user wants it, and run no reviewers and no `skill:` doc standard.
- Below regulated or contracted stakes, default and tag. At them, ask instead, and invent no regulation, citation, or threshold. An obligation the user did not name, from you or a reviewer, is an open question; headless, so is anything you would have asked.
- Only at launch, regulated, or contracted stakes may a review fix make the document longer.

## Ids and claims

Give stable ids (`PREFIX-N`) to commitments, assumptions, or items others will cite. Make commitments verifiable and assumptions explicit. Names, owners, dates, and company figures come from the user or the business record, never invented or inferred from the environment.

## Scope and open items

Keep the draft focused on the decision or audience at hand. A review finding that would widen the agreed scope goes to open items or a companion unless the user asks for it by name. Defer each open item with an owner and a revisit condition.
