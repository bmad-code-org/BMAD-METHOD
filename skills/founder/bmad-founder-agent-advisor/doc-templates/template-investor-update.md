# {title}

## Table of Contents

## Headline
<!-- Two or three lines: how the period went, good or bad, the one number that matters most, and the top ask. Write it last. When the period was bad, the headline says so. -->

## Metrics
<!-- A table of the same headline metrics as the last update: this period, last period, and the plan or target, each dated and matching `company.md`. A metric dropped from last time gets one line saying why. When the founder wants to swap a falling metric for a rising one, ask what readers will conclude when they notice. Done when every metric from last time is there or explained, and every figure matches the record. -->

## Wins
<!-- What went well, each with its effect: a customer signed and its value, a hire and the gap it fills, a launch and its first numbers. -->

## Misses
<!-- What went wrong or slipped, why, and what is being done. Founders want to leave these out, so ask directly. You are the investor who will find out anyway: ask what has gone worse than planned, and what the founder would not want readers to learn from someone else in six months. Done when each miss has a cause and a next step, and nothing that moved a headline metric down is missing. -->

## What we learned
<!-- What the period taught the founders about customers, the market or the product, and what they will do differently. One or two items with a consequence; skip lessons that changed nothing. -->

## Cash and runway
<!-- Cash in the bank, monthly net burn and runway in months and as the month it runs out, each dated, with the change from last period. Check the three agree; when they do not, ask which is right before writing any. Done when the runway date follows from cash and burn and matches `company.md`. -->

## Asks
<!-- What readers can do: an introduction to a named person or a described type, a hire, advice on a specific question. Each says who it is for and why. When an ask reads "help with sales" or "intros welcome", press until it names a person or a type of person. Done when a reader could act on each ask with one reply. -->

## Next period
<!-- optional. The two or three goals for next period, so the next update reports against them. -->
