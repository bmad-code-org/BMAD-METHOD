## Table of Contents

## Who it is for
<!-- The first group this launch reaches, narrow enough to say where they gather, and why them first. When the answer is "everyone" or a broad market, ask who would be most upset if the product disappeared, and start there. -->

## Message
<!-- The one line, the problem it solves in the customer's words, and the proof: a result, a quote, a demo. Ask where each phrase came from; a phrase no customer has used is a guess and gets tagged. -->

## What counts as working
<!-- The one number that shows the launch worked, its target, the date it is read, and what the founder will do if it is hit and if it is missed. When the target comes back as reach (visits, impressions, likes), ask what those people must do for it to matter. Done when the founder would accept a missed target as a reason to change course. -->

## Channels
<!-- One to three channels where this customer already is: a community, an email list, a marketplace, direct outreach, a launch site, a partner, paid ads. For each, the evidence it reaches them, the audience the founder has there today, and the cost. Ask which channel has already brought a customer; one never tried is a guess. Done when each channel has a reason grounded in this customer. -->
