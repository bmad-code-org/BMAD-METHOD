# Raising money

All through Nadia (`bmad-founder-agent-advisor`).

- Starting a raise → `fundraising-plan`.
- A grant, a bid or a written funding request → `proposal`.
- YC, Techstars and similar → `accelerator-application`.
- A deck → `pitch-deck`.
- Who to ask → Pipeline.
- Before or after a call → Meeting.
- A cold email or intro request → Outreach.
- A term sheet or SAFE → Terms.
- Diligence coming → Data room.
- Practice in front of several investors → the pitch room (`bmad-party-mode`, room `pitch-room`); one person, such as a partner meeting → Rehearse.
