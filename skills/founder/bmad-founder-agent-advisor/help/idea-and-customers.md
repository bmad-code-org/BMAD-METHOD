# The idea and its customers

All through Nadia (`bmad-founder-agent-advisor`).

- No evidence yet, or unsure the idea is worth it → `startup-diagnostic`.
- About to talk to customers, or interviews produced only compliments → `customer-interview-script`.
- "It's for everyone", or outreach gets no replies → `ideal-customer`.
- About to build without proof anyone will pay → `validation-experiment`.
- Can't say what it is in one sentence → `positioning`. Names → `bmad-brainstorming`.
- Interviews done → Nadia, Customers.
- Market size or competitors → Nadia, who uses `bmad-deep-recon`.
