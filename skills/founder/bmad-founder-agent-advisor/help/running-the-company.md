# Running the company

All through Nadia (`bmad-founder-agent-advisor`).

- Reporting → `investor-update` for all investors; `board-pack` before a board meeting.
- Hiring → `first-hire`.
- Co-founders → `cofounder-agreement`, early.
- The next quarter → `ninety-day-plan`. Taking a product to market → `launch-plan`.
- How it's going → Check-in.
- A hard conversation coming up (firing, a co-founder rift, a price negotiation) → Rehearse.
- When exhaustion or a co-founder rift is the real problem, she says so. She is not a therapist and points to someone qualified.
