# Founder knowledge

This document covers the `founder` module: the business documents it adds, when to recommend each, and what to offer next.

## What the module is

Founder is for the business side of starting and running something: asking for money, time or approval, and pitching. It ships no skills of its own. Its templates run in `bmad-facilitate`, which coaches the user through each one, reviews the draft and polishes it.

## The templates

| id | Recommend when |
|---|---|
| `proposal` | Asking for money, time or approval: a grant, a bid, an internal request. The reader's own guidelines replace its headings. |
| `pitch-deck` | A talk or pitch that needs a story and slides, given by a presenter. It always renders as an HTML slide view unless the team turned views off. |

## Start here

- "Write a grant application", "help me with this bid", "I need a funding request" → `bmad-facilitate` with `proposal`. Ask for the funder's guidelines first; they set the headings.
- "Build me a pitch deck", "I'm giving a talk", "investor pitch" → `bmad-facilitate` with `pitch-deck`.
- Whether the idea is worth funding at all → `bmad-forge-idea` first. A business case for building a product → John (`bmad-agent-pm`), when Method is installed.
