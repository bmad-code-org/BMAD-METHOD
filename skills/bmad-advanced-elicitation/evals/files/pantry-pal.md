# Pantry Pal — one-pager

## The problem

Busy parents with two jobs and kids at home do not have time to plan dinners. They end up buying takeout four nights a week, which costs money and isn't healthy.

## The product

Pantry Pal is a mobile app that plans your week of dinners. It shows you beautiful chef-curated recipes from around the world, lets you explore new cuisines, and saves the dishes you love. Every recipe has a step-by-step video and a wine pairing.

## Who it is for

Busy parents who want to get dinner on the table fast.

## MVP (6 weeks, one developer)

1. Recipe discovery feed
2. Cuisine explorer
3. Chef profiles
4. Step-by-step recipe videos
5. Wine pairings
6. Weekly meal plan
7. Shopping list
8. Pantry inventory with barcode scanning
9. Family member profiles with allergies
10. Social sharing
11. Grocery delivery integration
12. Nutrition dashboard

## Business model

Free tier with ads. Premium at $4.99/month removes ads and unlocks the meal planner. We expect 30% of free users to convert to premium in the first month.

## Competition

There is nothing else like Pantry Pal on the market today.

## Ask

$250k pre-seed to build the MVP and reach 10,000 users.
