Rate each strong, adequate, thin or broken, judged against the document's purpose and stakes:

1. Decision-ready: decisions and trade-offs stated; one clear thesis.
2. Earned: no persona, novelty, boilerplate-NFR or generic-vision theater; no padding.
3. Testable: every requirement can be checked; flag vague adjectives.
4. Honest scope: non-goals stated, inferences tagged, open items fit the stakes.
5. Usable downstream: consistent terms, ids resolve, depth and length fit the stakes.
6. Shape fit: the shape matches the product. Journeys carry a consumer, multi-stakeholder or UX-heavy product; a single-operator tool reads as a capability spec; a regulatory update traces its constraints; hobby work stays light. Flag both over- and under-formalizing.
