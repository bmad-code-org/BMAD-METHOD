## Slot and audience

Ten minutes at a product meetup in Leeds. Speaking rate not measured; 150 WPM assumed. [ASSUMPTION: 150 WPM]

## Core shift and open loop

Shift: a roadmap is a promise you make before you know enough. Open loop: "what did we put in its place?", closed in the last two minutes.

## Body beats and visual cues

1. The three quarters we missed in a row. [GAP: what actually happened, in their words]
2. The day we stopped. [GAP: the moment or meeting]
3. What replaced it: a list of problems with owners, reviewed every two weeks.
4. What got better, and what got worse.
