# Critique: "The Year the Lake Didn't Freeze" (opening)

3-second read: a cold, withheld opening; the reader does not yet know why the counting matters.

1. The first paragraph keeps the reader at arm's length. Consider letting one warm detail in so we care before the distance sets in.
2. "That was the arrangement." is strong; it could carry more weight as the paragraph's last line on its own.
3. The truck scene arrives without a turn; give it one change.
