notes for the villain, rough

- the Fog Collector. drifts in off the sea every autumn
- she gathers up lost sounds - foghorns, lullabies, a dog barking two towns over - and keeps them in jars
- wants: the one sound she's never caught, Old Burn's horn
- but if she takes it ships will wreck. she knows this. she doesn't want ships to wreck
- she's lonely, the jars are company
- looks like: a long grey coat that's sort of also the fog, pockets clinking with jars
- can: muffle anything, walk through walls of mist. can't: cross dry sand or go near the lamp's beam
- Wren is the only one who can hear her humming
- end idea (not sure): Wren trades her a new sound
