# Store Shift-Swap Tool Brief

Retail store associates need a way to handle shift swaps without texting managers at midnight:
1. An associate posts an open shift they cannot work.
2. Eligible peers in the same store claim the open shift.
3. The system sends SMS notifications when a shift is posted, claimed, or approved.
4. Store managers see pending swaps and approve or reject them.

Rollout plan: pilot in 12 stores during month 1, then roll out across all 48 stores.

Open questions not yet decided:
- Can associates swap a partial shift (e.g., 4 hours of an 8-hour shift) or only full shifts?
- If a manager does not respond within 24 hours, should a swap auto-approve or auto-expire?
