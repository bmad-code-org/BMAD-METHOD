# Personal Vinyl Tracker (Weekend Hobby Project)

I want a tiny weekend web app just for my own personal vinyl record collection at home (hobby stakes, keep it light — no formal PRD needed).

Features:
- Add records by artist, album, and pressing year.
- Search and filter my shelves by genre or artist.
- Track which record I spun last.

Success measure: I can look up whether I already own an album at a record shop in under 10 seconds on my phone.

Open details I haven't picked yet:
- Should I import my existing spreadsheet CSV or fetch metadata from Discogs?
- Light theme or dark theme?
