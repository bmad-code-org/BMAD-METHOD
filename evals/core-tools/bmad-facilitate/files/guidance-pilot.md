# Pilot planning guidance

These rules add to the engine's; the template's own comments win over them.

## Stakes

Read the stakes as one of: hobby or personal use, internal, pitch or investor, publication, public launch, regulated or contracted.

- At hobby stakes, one line fills a section. Skip reconciliation unless the user wants it, and run no reviewers and no `skill:` doc standard.
- Below regulated or contracted stakes, default and tag. At them, ask instead, and invent no regulation, citation, or threshold.
- Only at launch, regulated, or contracted stakes may a review fix make the document longer.

## Scope and ids

Give stable ids (`PREFIX-N`) to anything others will cite, make commitments verifiable, and keep names, owners, and dates sourced from the user. Defer open items with an owner and a revisit condition.
