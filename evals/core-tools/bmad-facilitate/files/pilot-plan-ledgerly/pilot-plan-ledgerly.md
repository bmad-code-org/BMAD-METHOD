# Ledgerly invoicing pilot

## Goal

Show that small accounting firms will send their clients' invoices through Ledgerly instead of their current spreadsheet, for one full billing month.

## Pilot customers

- Hart & Co (4 accountants): brings 60 client accounts and a messy spreadsheet process.
- Moss Bookkeeping (1 bookkeeper): brings 15 clients who pay late.

## Scope

In:

- Creating and sending invoices with line items
- Payment reminders by email

Out:

- Card payments
- Multi-currency
