John (`bmad-agent-pm`) helps you decide what to build, who it is for, and how it should work for users. He adapts to the size of your project: whether you have a small two-hour feature or a multi-team product rollout, he helps you get just enough clarity without burying you in paperwork.

Tell John what you want to achieve in plain words, or ask "John, what can you help me with?" to see everything he offers.

## What John Helps You Do

### Sharpen a small idea before building

Even if your change only takes a paragraph to describe, John can help you tighten the goal, spot edge cases, and write a crisp summary of what success looks like before you hand it off to get built.

### Plan a quick prototype or experiment

When you want to try an idea out quickly before committing to a full build, John helps you scope a lightweight prototype plan or a measured experiment with clear pass and fail thresholds.

### Shape a focused feature or product plan

For larger features or new products, John coaches you through user journeys, product rules, what is explicitly out of scope, and measurable goals. Once the plan takes shape, he pushes you to keep the first version small enough to ship and learn from quickly.

### Pitch a time-boxed cycle

If your team works in fixed cycles or bets on shaped pitches, John helps frame the problem, appetite, solution sketch, and rabbit holes to avoid.

### Coordinate multi-part products and programs

When several connected products or teams need to move together, John maps out shared outcomes, guardrails, and rollout order across the whole effort so individual teams stay aligned without micromanaging every screen.

### Write formal sign-off specs and launch plans

For regulated or contracted work that needs strict traceability, John writes detailed requirements specifications. When built work is ready to ship, he helps plan rollout steps, customer communication, and launch readiness across teams.

### Update or review an existing plan

Bring an existing product document, Confluence export, or draft, and John can revise it when scope changes or audit it read-only without modifying your files unless you ask.

:::note[Ways to start]
```text
/bmad-agent-pm I want to add self-serve team billing to our app. Help me work through what the first version should cover.
```

```text
/bmad-agent-pm Let's plan a quick prototype to test two different onboarding flows before we build the real thing.
```
:::

## Working with the Rest of the Team

- **Before John:** start with [Mary](mary-analyst.md) when you still need market research or a business case for why to build at all.
- **Alongside John:** pair with [Sally](sally-ux-designer.md) whenever visual direction, brand identity, or screen flows help shape the product.
- **After John:** hand a small, settled idea straight to [Amelia](amelia-dev.md) (`bmad-build`) to build, or bring a multi-part plan to [Sofia](sofia-po.md) to break down into buildable steps.

See [Write a PRD](../plan/write-a-prd.md) and [Plan Inside an Organization](../plan/plan-inside-an-organization.md) for walkthroughs.

## Tailoring John

To change how John prioritizes scope, add standing product rules, or customize what his documents cover, invoke `bmad-customize` and describe the change you want.
