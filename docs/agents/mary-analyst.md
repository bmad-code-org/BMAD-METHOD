Mary (`bmad-agent-analyst`) is your thinking partner before you commit time or money to building something. You do not need a business or software background to work with her: bring a rough hunch, messy notes, or a tough strategic question, and she helps you test whether the idea holds up and why.

Ask Mary in plain English, or ask "Mary, what can you help me with?" at any time to see everything she offers.

## What Mary Helps You Do

### Explore and brainstorm ideas

When you have a broad goal or feel stuck on where to start, Mary guides structured brainstorming sessions to push past the obvious first answers and uncover stronger angles.

### Research markets, competitors, and options

Mary runs live research on markets, competitors, domains, technologies, and customer feedback using current sources rather than guessing from memory. She can also compare competing tools or approaches side by side with clear evidence for each option.

### Pressure-test an idea before building

Before anyone writes a plan or a line of code, Mary helps you test whether a problem is worth solving. She can work backward from a mock launch announcement and tough customer questions to spot weak assumptions early, or map out your users' goals, frustrations, and motivations.

### Make the case to stakeholders

When a manager, sponsor, or team needs to sign off on funding or direction, Mary helps you write clear, candid decision documents: short product briefs, financial business cases with costs and expected returns, leadership strategy narratives, program charters, and formal business requirements documents when an outside team or regulated environment needs sign-off.

### Design small experiments

Instead of betting everything on a big build, Mary helps you frame a focused test of one risky assumption with clear pass and fail criteria agreed before you run it.

### Keep your project's AI instructions sharp

Mary can set up or refresh your repository's `AGENTS.md` instructions so coding agents understand your real build commands, conventions, and pitfalls without bloating the file.

:::note[Ways to start]
```text
/bmad-agent-analyst Help me research whether small clinics already pay for automated appointment follow-ups.
```

```text
Hey Mary, I want to build an internal portal for onboarding partners. Help me write a short brief and business case.
```
:::

## Working with the Rest of the Team

Once you decide an idea is worth pursuing, Mary hands off cleanly depending on what you need next:

- **Plan the product:** talk to [John](john-pm.md) for any shape of product planning, from tightening a small idea or planning a quick prototype to writing a full feature or program plan.
- **Explore the user experience first:** talk to [Sally](sally-ux-designer.md) to map user psychology, brand identity, or screen flows before locking requirements.
- **Turn a settled brief into buildable work:** take your brief or business case straight to [Sofia](sofia-po.md) when you already know what to build.
- **Plan a startup or raise:** if you are starting or funding a company outside a product codebase, work with [Nadia](../founder/founder.md) instead.

See [Clarify the Idea](../clarify/clarify-the-idea.md) and [Plan Inside an Organization](../plan/plan-inside-an-organization.md) for how discovery fits into a project.

## Tailoring Mary

To adjust Mary's tone, standing project context, or extra guidelines, invoke `bmad-customize` and describe what you want changed.
