Clio (`bmad-creative-agent-director`) is built around one core promise: anything published under your name should come from your own ideas and sound like you, not a generic language model.

## Capture Your Real Voice

Instead of describing your style with vague adjectives like "punchy" or "conversational", Clio builds a voice bible from 3 to 5 real pieces or talk transcripts you actually wrote or spoke. She quotes your real openings, transitions, sentence rhythm, and natural filler words to keep versus cut, and calculates your real speaking speed (words per minute) when transcripts include durations.

You can keep your voice bible inside one project or save it in your home folder so your personal voice carries across every project you open.

## Braindump First, Then Weave in Your Own Words

When writing a talk, essay, or script from scratch, Clio separates gathering raw thoughts from polishing prose:

1. **Braindump:** Clio interviews you one focused question at a time (or files an existing voice memo or transcript untouched), keeping your exact phrasing, slang, and half-formed sparks intact.
2. **Outline approval:** Before drafting prose, she proposes three distinct opening hooks and a beat-by-beat outline tied directly to lines from your braindump, flagging any gaps where real proof is still missing.
3. **Quote-or-Cut drafting:** She weaves the draft from your braindump phrasing, writes the opening hook last once the body proves it, and visibly tags any synthetic bridge sentence she had to invent. If more than a quarter of the draft is synthetic filler, she stops and asks for real material instead of padding.

## Automatic Cliché and Pacing Checks

Before presenting woven prose, revised drafts, titles, or spoken scripts, Clio runs automated checks on the text:

- **AI tell and cliché scan:** catches common AI buzzwords, stiff essay transitions, and spoken teleprompter trip-ups like em-dashes and en-dashes on scripts meant to be read aloud.
- **Spoken runtime and rhythm:** estimates real speaking duration at your measured pace (skipping notes and tables of contents on talk scripts), counts short punchy lines, and warns if long sentences pile up in a row without breathing room.

## Visuals, Slide Critique, and Live Rehearsal

- **Visual escalation and slide critique:** turns dense prose into clear diagrams, comparisons, or editable sketches, and audits existing slide decks for 3-second readability, strong assertion headlines, and zero walls of bullets.
- **Titles and hooks:** generates distinct, non-obvious title and hook options across safe and bold angles, then checks that your actual draft delivers on every promise each title makes.
- **Talk rehearsal:** roleplays a skeptical audience Q&A after checking spoken timing and tongue-twisting phrases, then debriefs where pushback hit hardest.

## Make Feedback Stick Across Sessions

Whenever you spot an off-voice phrase, a cheesy trope, or a direction you want retired for good, tell Clio. Instead of fixing it only in chat (where the lesson disappears next session), she records dated rules into your voice bible, cliché list, or canon so every future draft starts sharper.

## Creative Studio Rooms

When a piece needs more than one perspective, Clio can convene three multi-voice rooms:

- **The Creative Studio:** six distinct creative lenses debating your concept from competing angles, remembering insights across sessions.
- **The Midnight Salon:** wild, unexpected divergence alongside historical and playful guests, tested against a candid real-world client before closing.
- **The Critique Table:** a focused pre-ship editorial and visual roast to sharpen a nearly finished piece.
