Beyond writing business plans and updates, Nadia (`bmad-founder-agent-advisor`) runs exact financial math, turns messy notes into investor-ready evidence, and lets you rehearse tough conversations before they count.

## Exact Financial Math

Estimating runway, dilution, or unit economics in prose leads to quiet math mistakes that hurt credibility. Nadia computes every financial model through a deterministic script and saves both the inputs and monthly spreadsheets into your business record:

- **Company and personal runway:** month-by-month cash burn, default-alive check, and how long personal savings last if you leave a day job (keeping a safety buffer).
- **Unit economics and market size:** customer lifetime value, acquisition payback, and bottom-up market sizing from real account counts and annual prices.
- **SAFEs and priced rounds:** ownership and dilution after post-money SAFEs convert or after a priced round closes.
- **Monthly operating models:** dated hiring plans, recurring costs, and one-off expenses exported as clean spreadsheets.

## Customer Evidence and Strategic Calls

- **Customer synthesis:** drop raw interview transcripts or notes in and Nadia extracts what people already pay for today, recurring patterns, and whether the evidence supports or weakens your riskiest assumption.
- **Big decisions with kill criteria:** structure decisions like quitting a job, pivoting, or hiring with explicit evidence targets and a future check-in date so you revisit them honestly later.
- **Weekly or milestone check-ins:** review what moved, what stalled, and any open commitments due for follow-up.

## Fundraising Pipeline, Terms, and Diligence

- **Investor targets and outreach:** build and rank a list of investors or early customers, prep before calls, debrief after, draft cold emails or forwardable warm-intro blurbs, and file raw emails or metrics exports cleanly.
- **Term sheets in plain English:** read a SAFE or term sheet with Nadia to spot what is standard, what is unusual, how it changes your ownership, and which questions to bring to your lawyer.
- **Data room readiness:** audit what investors at your stage will ask to see and spot missing or out-of-date items before diligence starts.

## Live Rehearsal and Pitch Room

- **One-on-one rehearsal:** ask Nadia to play the other person across the table (an investor partner, a customer negotiating price, or a co-founder in a hard conversation) and debrief what landed afterwards.
- **Investor pitch room:** pitch to a full room of distinct investor personas grounded in your real company record. They grill your numbers and thesis, make offers or pass, and automatically save the hardest questions they asked to your objection bank so your next pitch is sharper.

:::caution[Boundaries]
Nadia is not a lawyer, accountant, licensed financial adviser, or therapist. On incorporation, contracts, tax, securities, or term sheets she gives you the exact questions to take to qualified counsel; when founder exhaustion or co-founder conflict goes beyond business advice, she names it plainly and points you to qualified support.
:::
